# Pitch facts: what Autinerary is, in numbers and differences

For pitches, applications and conversations with partners. Every number has a
date; check the live figure before quoting it in anything formal. Last
updated 18 October 2026.

## One line

Autinerary turns "what do I need?" into a plan and a team: a planning app and
a service directory built for neurodivergent people and the families and
supporters around them, free during the beta.

## The numbers (18 October 2026)

| What | How many |
| --- | --- |
| Places listed in ResourceHub | 35,938, most in Ontario (25,725 Ontario places published 18 October) |
| Services checked by hand on their own websites | 649: 364 in nine lists (funding, newcomers, young children, neurodivergent mentoring and tutoring, siblings, 20 universities, 45 colleges), and 285 from the Canadian National Autism Foundation's pages, in every province and territory and nationally |
| Ratings from the community | 521 |
| Colleges and universities with campus pages | 47 |
| Kinds of service and education tags | 95, plus 43 fields of post-secondary study |

## What it does

- **Three clicks to a team**: type what you need in a sentence, press Enter,
  add the best match to your team with a first meeting in your calendar, and
  sign them on.
- **A plan, not just a list**: goals become steps, written with AI and
  labelled as such, scheduled in a calendar, with check-ins and reflections.
- **Ratings you can trust**: only people who used a service can rate it; a
  monthly sweep warns, then hides, services rated low; nothing is deleted.
- **Community's Choice**: the community nominates and votes each month, and
  for the year. **Users' Choice** shows what each type of user rated highest:
  people who share a norm, families, professionals.
- **Products checked for what matters**: the shop lists products people
  recommend, in 40 categories, each with the qualities people with sensory
  and other needs look for (tagless, fragrance-free, turns down noise, by a
  neurodivergent author...), each quality checked on the maker's own pages.
- **Accessible from the start**: plain language, colour themes and colour
  strength, text size, reduced motion, "Listen to this page" everywhere,
  automated WCAG 2.1 AA checks across both apps on phone and desktop, and
  the help lines up front (911, 9-8-8).
- **Services keep their own listing right**: a Resource Account, checked by a
  code sent to the listing's own email; they see counts, never who.
- **Honest by design**: nothing is called autism-friendly unless the place or
  the people who used it say so; we don't sell information or show ads.

## What makes it different

- Built with and for neurodivergent people and their families, from Riipen
  Labs student groups' research (`docs/groups-*.md`), parents' feedback and
  the autism community.
- Both the plan and the people: most tools do one or the other.
- Free-or-paid, distance, "requires a diagnosis", funding help and newcomer
  services on every listing.
- Free during the beta; after it, a free first month with no card, then
  monthly or yearly, and free for many through universities, workplaces and
  governments (the #FundAutinerary campaign; the Khan Academy model).
