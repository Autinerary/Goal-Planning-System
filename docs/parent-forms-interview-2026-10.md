# Which forms are worst? A guide for talking with parents, October 2026

Goal 2 of the planning note: before building anything that fills in forms,
talk with parents (the connections in Jamaica, Nobellum, others) "to see which
forms are the worst". This is a guide for those conversations, about 15
minutes each, and how to write the answers down so they can be compared.

## Before you start

- Say what it is for: "We want to cut the paperwork families do. Which forms
  take you the longest, and which ask the same things again and again?"
- Say what we won't do: we won't fill in or send anything for them, and they
  don't need to show us any form or share anything private.
- Ask if you may take notes. Don't record names in the notes; use "Parent A",
  "Parent B".

## Questions

1. Think of the last year. Which forms did you fill in for your child (or
   yourself)? Let them list; then show the list below and ask which they had.
2. Which one took the longest? Roughly how long, including getting documents?
3. Which ones asked for the same things as another form? Which things?
4. Which needed a doctor, psychologist or teacher to fill in a part? How long
   did you wait for that?
5. Did any get sent back, or refused, because something was missing or wrong?
6. Which were on paper, which online, which either?
7. If one form could be done for you up to the signature, which would you
   choose?
8. Would you trust Autinerary to keep your details to fill in forms, if you
   checked every answer before anything was sent? What would make you trust
   it more?
9. Anything else that would have made it easier?

## Forms to show them

Ontario and Canada:

- Ontario Autism Program registration (AccessOAP)
- Developmental Services Ontario application
- Special Services at Home, Passport, and ACSD applications
- ODSP application
- Disability Tax Credit (T2201) and the Child Disability Benefit
- RDSP opening
- OSAP Disability Verification Form, and the Bursary for Students with
  Disabilities
- A college's or university's accessibility intake form
- School board requests for an IPRC or IEP, and special education forms
- Assistive Devices Program forms

Jamaica: ask them to name theirs (school, Ministry of Education, Early
Childhood Commission, health, and benefit forms).

## Write down, for each parent

| Form | Had it? | Time it took | Repeats which other form | Needs a professional's part | Sent back? | Paper or online | Would choose it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## After five or more conversations

Rank the forms by how many parents chose them (question 7), then by time.
The top three to five are the first to build "fill once, reuse" for
(`docs/contracting-2026-10.md`, questions 2 and 3).
