# Taking part in Autinerary beta testing

Please read this before your session. It takes about 4 minutes. If anything
is unclear, ask us: aayush@autinerary.ca

## What this is

Autinerary is a free app that turns a goal into a step-by-step plan and helps
you find services and places that fit how you work. It is in beta, which
means it is still being improved. We are testing the app, **not you**. There
are no wrong answers, and anything that is hard to do is the app's fault.

## What you would do

**In a session** (about 40 minutes, on a video call):

- You use the app on your own phone or computer and share your screen.
- We ask you to try seven things, for example make an account, find a
  service near you, and rate a place you've been to.
- While you do them, you say out loud what you are thinking, what you
  expect, and what surprises you.
- After each one, we ask how easy or hard it was.

**In the 30-day trial** (12 October to 10 November): you use Autinerary as
much or as little as you like. Now and then the app asks one short question,
like whether it is useful so far. You can skip any of them.

## Your choices

- Taking part is up to you. You can stop at any time, skip any task or
  question, and take a break whenever you want, without giving a reason.
- **Recording is optional.** You can say yes or no below, and still take
  part either way. You can keep your camera off.
- If you change your mind after the session, email us and we will remove
  your session notes and any recording.

## What we keep, and how we use it

- **From a session:** the facilitator's notes (what was easy or hard, and
  how long each task took), what you say, and a screen recording if you
  agree to it.
- **From the app:** what Autinerary records for everyone, described at
  https://goal-planning-app.vercel.app/privacy
- Notes and recordings are labelled with a code, like P07, not your name.
  Your name and email are kept separately and only used to arrange things
  with you.
- Only the Autinerary team sees notes and recordings. We use them to find
  and fix what is confusing. Reports show totals, not individual people,
  and any quote we share is anonymous and only with your permission below.
- **We do not use what you say or do in testing to train AI.**
- Recordings are deleted when we have finished going through them, and by
  31 December 2026 at the latest.

## Is there any risk?

Some tasks may be frustrating, or you may find talking while you work
tiring. You can take a break or stop at any time. You don't have to share
anything about your health or diagnosis. If you do, it is kept under your
code only.
