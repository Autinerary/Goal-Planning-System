# Outreach: posts and messages

October is ADHD Awareness Month (Autism Acceptance Month is April), so the
posts lead with that. Each channel has its own link, so the funnel report can
compare channels. Use the link for the place you are posting, exactly as
written.

## Links

Two links per post: one to try the app, one to sign up for a session.

Riipen Labs' Group 4 recommended sending each campaign "to targeted landing
paths instead of a general destination". So each link opens **Start here**
(`/start`): two quick questions, then a few starter resources and "Save this
path", which is the free account. Where a channel mostly reaches one kind of
person, its link answers the first question for them (`for=`), and the
schools link answers both (`need=`). People can change either answer.

To make another targeted link, add to `/start?`:

- `for=` who they are here for: `self`, `child`, `family` (family member or
  friend), `work` (someone they teach, support or work with), `ally`
- `need=` what they need today: `learn` (starter information), `services`,
  `community` (people with similar experiences), `school_work`, `sensory`

Leave both out when a post reaches mixed groups: a wrong first answer is
worse than one more question.

Riipen Labs' Group 5 found that adults from fast channels (TikTok, Reddit)
look for "peer tools", so those links open on people with similar
experiences (Tidbits first). Parents from Facebook look for "trust & safety",
so theirs opens on starter information, with a line on privacy under "Save
this path".

For print (merch, the comic, research posts) and anywhere a long link is
awkward, use the short links and QR codes in
[../campaign/README.md](../campaign/README.md), for example
`app.autinerary.ca/c/comic`. They are tracked the same way. The home page address
(`https://app.autinerary.ca/?utm_source=...`) still works, and
also starts with Start here.

Session sign-up form: [[TEAM: Google Form link, once the screener is made]]

Never add anything personal to a link (no names or emails). To tell two
posts apart on the same channel, change only the campaign, for example
`utm_campaign=beta-oct-2026-reel`.

Before posting anywhere:

- Write alt text for every image (examples below).
- Write hashtags in CamelCase (#ADHDAwarenessMonth), so screen readers read
  them as words.
- No flashing or fast-moving visuals.

## Reddit

ask whether a beta-tester post is welcome. Only post where they say yes, and
use the community's flair for research or feedback if it has one.

> **Title:** [Beta testers wanted] A free planning app built for
> neurodivergent people, 40-minute session or 30-day trial (18+)
>
