# Session script (facilitator)

About 40 minutes on a video call. Say the parts in quotes out loud; the rest
is for you. Facilitator: [[TEAM: name]].

## Before the session (10 minutes)

- [ ] The tester's code (P01, P02, ...) is in the research spreadsheet.
- [ ] They answered the four questions in [consent.md](consent.md). If not,
      ask them at the start (step 1 below).
- [ ] You know what helps them from the screener (question 8): captions,
      written instructions, camera off, breaks, more time.
- [ ] Have the tasks below ready to paste into the chat, so they can read
      each one as well as hear it.
- [ ] Open a copy of the notes template (end of this file), named with
      their code, never their name.
- [ ] Only press record if they said yes to recording.

## 1. Welcome (3 minutes)

"Thanks for doing this. We're testing the app, not you. If something is hard,
that's the app's fault and exactly what we want to find.

I'll ask you to try seven things. While you do, please say out loud what
you're thinking: what you're looking for, what you expect, what surprises
you. It feels odd at first, that's normal.

I won't help much, because we want to see where the app gets in the way. If
you're stuck I'll step in. You can take a break or stop at any time, and skip
anything."

If they haven't answered the consent questions yet: "Before we start: are
you 18 or older? Have you read the description and want to take part? May I
record your screen and voice? May we share anonymous quotes?" Record the
answers in the notes.

If recording: "I'm starting the recording now."

## 2. The tasks (about 30 minutes)

Paste each task into the chat as you read it. Start the timer when they
start. Don't explain the app.

**If they are stuck** for about two minutes, or ask for help, step in a
little at a time and note it as "with help":

1. "What would you try next?"
2. "Is there anywhere on this screen it might be?"
3. Show them, and move on.

**After every task** ask: "How easy or hard was that, from 1, very hard, to
5, very easy?" Then: "Anything you expected to be different?"

| # | Read this | Success means | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Make an account and get to your plan." | Reaches the Path, with email or "Sign in with Google" | Which sign-up they pick; the date-of-birth question; which of the three setup steps slows them down; whether they understand "norms" |
| 2 | "What is the first thing your plan suggests you do?" | Says the first milestone | Whether the first screen makes the next step obvious |
| 3 | "Find a service near you that could help with that." | Opens a relevant ResourceHub result | Whether they find ResourceHub; the location question; whether the results feel relevant |
| 4 | "Mark something as done." | Completes a milestone or task | What they expect to happen after; how they feel about the celebration |
| 5 | "Make the app easier on your eyes." | Changes a display setting | Where they look first; whether they find Settings |
| 6 | "You want the app to stop sending you emails. Do that." | Finds the email settings | Whether they expect it in Settings or in an email |
| 7 | "Find a place you've been to, or would like to go, and rate it." | Submits a rating | Whether the rating questions make sense; whether rating by norms feels useful |

Ratings matter beyond the session: the norm and rating filters in
ResourceHub only work once people have rated real places.

## 3. Wrap-up (5 minutes)

Ask, and write their words down as closely as you can:

1. "What would you use this for, if anything?"
2. "What nearly made you give up?"
3. "What was the most confusing moment?"
4. "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
5. "How likely are you to keep using it, from 1, not at all, to 5, very?"

"Thank you, this really helps. [[TEAM: Thank-you, if any, and how it's
sent.]] If you'd like to keep going, you're welcome in the 30-day trial
until 10 November; we'll email you the link. If you think of anything later,
email aayush@autinerary.ca."

Stop the recording.

## 4. Afterwards (within the hour)

- Finish the notes while it's fresh. Use the code, never their name.
- Save the recording, if any, under the code, in the team's restricted
  folder.
- Add a row to the research spreadsheet: code, group (who they're here
  for), neurodivergent or not (from the screener), and each task's success
  and ease.
